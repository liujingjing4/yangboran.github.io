## About Me
I am an Associate Professor and Master Supervisor at the School of Liangjiang Artificial Intelligence, Chongqing University of Technology. I received my Ph.D. degree from Chongqing University of Posts and Telecommunications.

My research interests include **Social Intelligence, Ubiquitous Networks, and AIoT Security**. I have published more than 30 technical papers in top journals such as IEEE JSAC, TWC, TCSVT, TMM, TASLP, IoTJ, etc. I have been awarded Best Paper Awards at IEEE MSN 2020 and IEEE GreenCom 2019. I serve as Associate Editor, Technical Editor, and Guest Editor for several prestigious journals, and act as a reviewer for many top IEEE transactions.
